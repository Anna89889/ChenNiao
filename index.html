✨ About the Author

Chen Niao is a Chinese poet and essayist whose work focuses on memory, silence, and emotional perception.
Her writing moves between poetry and prose, capturing the subtle emotions hidden within everyday life.
She believes writing is not only expression—but also a way of remembering what time tries to erase.
