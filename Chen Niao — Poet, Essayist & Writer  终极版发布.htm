散文集 · Essay Collection (Chinese)

那天，那人，那眼神

有些记忆会消退，而有些记忆则会静静留在心中。这本散文集记录曾经遇见的人、分享过的瞬间，以及那些无法忘记的目光。在时间的流动中，它试图捕捉人心留下的痕迹。

About the Author

Chen Niao · 晨鸟

Chen Niao is a Chinese poet and essayist whose work focuses on memory, silence, and emotional perception. Her writing moves between poetry and prose, capturing the subtle emotions hidden within everyday life.

“Writing is not only expression — but also a way of remembering what time tries to erase.”

Chen Niao — writing about memory, silence, and human emotion.
